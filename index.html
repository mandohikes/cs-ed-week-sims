Computer Science Education Week 2026

Interactive simulations for Mississippi classrooms · December 7-11, 2026 · MSU Center for Cyber Education

Pick your grade band, then pick a simulation. Every simulation has two modes: Teacher-Led for the projector and Student-Led for individual devices. No sound is needed, and every sim has a small Teacher button in the corner to jump, skip, or reset.

K-2

What Is a Computer? (Version 1)Meet the parts, sort computers from non-computers, build a computer, superstar quiz. What Is a Computer? (Version 2)Tap the parts, match each part to its job, input or output game. Computer Parts ParadeParts march by: part or not a part, feed the computer, sticker scene.

Grades 3-5

Program a Path (Version 1)Arrow-block programming on a grid, debug, shortest program wins. Program a Path (Version 2)Draw target pictures with arrow and fill commands. Robot DeliveryProgram a delivery robot through town, with a repeat (loop) block.

Grades 6-8

Train a Machine (Version 1)Build a training set, test your model, catch the bias. Train a Machine (Version 2)Sort pets to train, watch a lopsided dataset fail, fix it and re-run. Spam Filter TrainerTrain a spam filter, catch the false positive, fix the training data.

Grades 9-12

Defend the School (Version 1)Inbox triage, red-flag hunt, phishing beyond email. Defend the School (Version 2)Inspect sender, link, and tone; then work incident cards. Real or AI?Spot AI-generated media, verify before you share.
Built by the MSU Center for Cyber Education for Mississippi CS Education Week, December 7-11, 2026.
